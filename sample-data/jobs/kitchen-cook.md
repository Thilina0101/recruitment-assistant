# Kitchen Cook / พนักงานครัว — Karena Curry Rice

Karena is a small Japanese curry rice shop in Bangkok. We're looking for a cook to join our kitchen team.

## What you'll do
- Prepare Japanese curry, rice, katsu (fried pork/chicken cutlets) and side dishes to our recipes
- Keep the kitchen clean and follow food safety rules
- Prepare ingredients before opening and check stock
- Work quickly during the lunch and dinner rush (up to 150 orders a day)

## Required
- At least 1 year of experience working in a restaurant or commercial kitchen
- Experience deep-frying (katsu, tempura, karaage or similar)
- Knows food hygiene and safety practices
- Can work shifts 10:00–21:00 including weekends (5 days a week)
- Can communicate in Thai at a basic level

## Nice to have
- Experience with Japanese food
- Food handler / food safety training certificate
- Experience in a high-volume or fast-service restaurant
- Can help with stock ordering

## Pay and benefits
- 13,000–16,000 THB per month depending on experience, plus service charge
- Staff meals, uniform, 6 days annual leave after probation

## How to apply
Send your resume via LINE @karena or come to the shop.
